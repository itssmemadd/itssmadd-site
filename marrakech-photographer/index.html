Why shoot in Marrakech

Marrakech is one of the most photogenic cities on earth — ochre walls, carved doors, rooftop sunsets and desert light 40 minutes away. As a Marrakech photographer, I know where the light falls and when the crowds thin out, so your photos feel effortless, never touristy.

Every booking includes location guidance: tell me the mood you want, and I'll match it — from a quiet riad courtyard to the dunes of Agafay.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a photographer in Marrakech cost?

Rates depend on the type and length of the shoot — a portrait session, a full wedding day and an event report are priced differently. Contact me for a tailored quote based on your date and needs.

How far in advance should I book a Marrakech photoshoot?

Two to four weeks in advance is ideal, especially in spring and autumn when Marrakech is busiest. Last-minute requests are welcome when the calendar allows.

Do you shoot outside Marrakech?

Yes. I'm based in Agadir and travel across Morocco — including the Agafay desert, Essaouira and the Atlas mountains — for weddings, editorials and events.

How do I receive my photos?

You receive a private online gallery of edited, high-resolution images, ready to print and share — usually within one week of the shoot.

Can we shoot at specific Marrakech locations?

Absolutely — the medina, Jardin Majorelle, riad rooftops, the Palmeraie or the Agafay desert. Location suggestions are part of every booking.
